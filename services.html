Our Equipment

World-Class Diagnostic Machines

We use advanced, internationally recognized diagnostic machines to ensure accurate and reliable test results for every patient.

🇯🇵 Japan

Thyroid & Immunoassay

For thyroid and immunoassay tests, we use Tosoh AiA 360 a world class Japanese analyzer with exceptional sensitivity and precision.

🇸🇪 Sweden

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

For CBC tests, we use Medonic M Series a world class Sweden-made hematology analyzer trusted in hospitals globally.

🇩🇪 Germany

Biochemistry Tests

Liver, kidney, and lipid profile tests are performed using Erba Chem 7 & Erba Chem 6 Germany manufactured clinical chemistry analyzers.

🇯🇵 Japan

Cancer & Tumor Markers

Cancer and tumor marker tests are conducted using Tosoh AiA 360 Immunoassay Analyzer delivering highly accurate oncology results.

🇮🇳 India

Electrolyte Analysis

For electrolyte imbalance testing, we use a Micro Lab Electrolyte Analyzer a world class Indian manufactured system for sodium, potassium, and chloride levels.
