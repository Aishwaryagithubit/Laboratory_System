Who We Are

Accurate Diagnostics, Compassionate Care

Hi-Tech Pathology Lab was founded with a single mission to bring accurate, timely, and affordable diagnostic services to the people of Janakpurdham and the wider Dhanusha district of Nepal.

We combine highly qualified medical professionals with modern diagnostic equipment to ensure every patient receives results they can trust quickly and reliably.

  • NHPC registered lab technicians & professionals
  • Modern equipment with regular quality checks
  • Fast turnaround most reports same day
  • Affordable pricing with home collection available
  • Free health camps & community outreach programs
